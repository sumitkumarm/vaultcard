# Card list network marks

The Visa and Mastercard PNG assets identify the network of a saved card. They are bundled locally and rendered in their original colors, without stretching or recoloring.

Source artwork, retrieved October 25, 2026:

- Visa: https://www.mycardwallet.com/chs/mygift/assets/shared/logo-visa-alt.webp
- Mastercard: https://www.mycardwallet.com/chs/mcgift/assets/shared/logo-mastercard.webp

The issuer's WebP artwork was converted to PNG without modifying its content. Visa and Mastercard retain ownership of their respective marks.
